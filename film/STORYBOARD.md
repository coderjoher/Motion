# Jafer Nouri: "First message" (motionmaxxing film)

## PAGE
- **Problem:** a small business in Iraq has no proper website, and certainly not one that reads naturally in Arabic *and* English. Customers can't find it or reach it.
- **Audience:** business owners in a muted Instagram / LinkedIn feed, bilingual (Arabic + English). They will give the film 20 s at most.
- **Goal:** message Jafer.
- **Emotion:** from "a website is a big, vague project" to "one message, and it's live in both languages. That could be mine."

**The one true thing:** Jafer's own process line, "From first message to live site", and the detail only he leads with: Arabic pages are built right-to-left from the start, "so they read naturally instead of feeling like a translation" (his FAQ).

## Ideas
1. *First idea (discarded):* laptop mock-ups fly around under "Design that delivers results." This is the category costume, and any agency could post it.
2. *Montage of real output:* his four real client builds cut to a rhythm. It's real, but four covers alone don't say what is different about him.
3. **UI documentary of one real task (chosen):** a first message is typed into his chat. The camera dives into the sent message and it becomes the first wireframe block. The wireframe becomes his real sample site. His cursor presses `العربية` and the whole page **mirrors**: every block crosses to its right-to-left place and arrives in real Arabic. The site goes live, a visitor presses `راسلنا` (Message us), and the enquiries counter goes 0 → 1: the site's *first message*. A whip pan through his four real builds lands on his name.

*Why:* the bilingual mirror is the thing he sells that a competitor's reel doesn't show, and the film starts and ends on a message (his core control). **Cover-the-logo:** a generic agency could not run the Arabic mirror beat with their own Arabic copy. **Competitor swap:** the copy, the sample site, the projects and the face are all his.

## Checks before building
- Lengths: 105/120/135/86/74/70 f = 590 f (19.7 s). The CTA is 70 f = 12 % (≤ 25 %), and the planned end hold is ~1.0 s.
- G1: page text is magnified by the camera (macro on the input, toggle, Arabic headline and counter), and the page is ≥ 0.75 W at its widest.
- G4: one hero per frame. The chat panel, then the page, then one cover at a time, then the lockup.
- Chrome audit: no corner labels, counters, timecodes or progress bars. The URL bar, chips and annotation labels are inside the rebuilt page (`data-ui="captured"`).
- Invented or illustrative: the client's message text, the 0 → 1 count, and the visitor press. Everything else on screen is lifted from the live site.
